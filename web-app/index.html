Monthly Budget How spending is calculated:
Amounts shown are net: for each category, any refunds (income of the same category in the same month) are subtracted.

Example: spent 50 € on Restaurants, reimbursed 10 € → net spending = 40 €.

The Investments category is excluded from the total.
Net spending never goes below zero.
